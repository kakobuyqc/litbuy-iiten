A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and compare details such as category, sizing, listed price, and available photos. Use it to build a shortlist; a spreadsheet entry does not verify quality, authenticity, stock, or compatibility with a shopping platform.

A useful list needs a useful process

How to use a
Litbuy spreadsheet.

Turn an interesting link into a considered choice. Keep your own comparison notes so that a familiar image or a low listed price does not become the whole decision.

Build your shortlist ↗
  1. 01

    Choose the category first.

    Start with Litbuy spreadsheet clothing or Litbuy spreadsheet shoes. Narrow by garment type, silhouette, material, and the size information you need.

  2. 02

    Read the exact product details.

    Record the original product URL, selected color, size, listed price, and seller notes. Similar photos can appear on listings with different specifications.

  3. 03

    Compare fit and available QC photos.

    For Litbuy spreadsheet finds, compare measurements with an item you own. Review available quality-control photos for visible condition and variant accuracy; photos alone do not prove authenticity.

  4. 04

    Check compatibility and the full estimate.

    Confirm whether your chosen platform accepts the original link. Review item cost, applicable fees, shipping estimates, and destination charges before making a payment.

Before you choose

A few clear
answers.

A practical reference for understanding the directory, checking product information, and using a shortlist thoughtfully.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and compare details such as category, sizing, listed price, and available photos. A spreadsheet entry does not verify quality, authenticity, stock, or platform compatibility.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and shopping research guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS pages rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

How do I check whether a product link works with Litbuy?

Check the current instructions on the platform you intend to use and test the original product URL there before paying. This directory does not confirm that a CAFANS page or any particular listing is supported.

What should I compare before choosing clothing or sneakers?

Compare the exact variant, measurements, materials, available QC photos, seller terms, and total estimated cost. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own. For shoes, check the listing's sizing guidance and available length measurements.

Can QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible condition, color, stitching, and the selected variant, but they do not establish authenticity. For authenticity assurance, use an authorized retailer or a qualified authentication service.

Are the displayed illustrations live product listings?

No. The illustrations are editorial category references. They do not represent verified inventory, official brand partnerships, current prices, or authenticated products.
